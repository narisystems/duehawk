Privacy Policy

Your passports, licences and policies are yours alone. Here's exactly what DueHawk stores, where, and what never leaves your phone.

👁 Overview

DueHawk is a local-first tracker for things that expire: insurance policies, passports, licences, vehicle registrations, warranties and memberships. It does not require an account with NaRiSystems, and NaRiSystems runs no servers that receive your records.

✅ Bottom line: NaRiSystems does not collect, store, or sell your items, notes, dates or attached documents. They stay on your device, plus your own Google Drive if you turn on backup.

This policy covers the only cases where anything leaves your device: the optional Google Drive backup (Premium), anonymous crash reports, and purchases handled by Google Play. DueHawk contains no advertising and no usage analytics.

📱 Local Storage & Permissions

Your items, reminders, settings and attachments are stored in DueHawk's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Android's own automatic cloud backup is switched off for DueHawk, so this data is not copied anywhere unless you turn on DueHawk's Google Drive backup.

DueHawk asks for these permissions, each used only for the purpose shown:

  • Camera — to photograph a document you want to attach. Photos are saved inside the app, not to your gallery.
  • Notifications and alarms & reminders — to remind you before something expires, at the time you chose.
  • Run at startup — to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Biometrics — to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face if you enable app lock. The check happens inside Android; DueHawk only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Internet — only for Google Drive backup, Google Play purchases, and crash reports.

When you pick a photo or PDF from your files, DueHawk copies only that file into its private storage. It does not scan or read anything else on your device.

☁️ Optional Google Drive Backup

Premium users can back up to their own Google Drive. Backup is off until you sign in with Google and turn it on.

  • DueHawk requests only the drive.appdata permission. This gives it access to a hidden folder reserved for DueHawk in your Drive, and not to any of your other Drive files.
  • A backup contains your items, categories, settings and attached files.
  • DueHawk keeps your 5 most recent backups and automatically deletes older ones.
  • Backups are protected by your Google account's security. DueHawk does not add its own encryption to them, so keep your Google account secure.
  • NaRiSystems cannot see, access or recover your backups.
ℹ️ To delete your backups, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → DueHawk → Delete hidden app data. You can also disconnect DueHawk from your Google account at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps.

💳 Purchases

DueHawk Premium is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play. NaRiSystems never receives your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Premium, so it can unlock features and restore your purchase after a reinstall. Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

📈 Crash Reporting, Analytics & Advertising

DueHawk uses Sentry to receive a report when the app crashes or hits an error, so we can fix it. A report contains technical details only: the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, device model and Android version.

  • DueHawk never adds your item names, notes, dates or attachments to a report.
  • No screenshots, screen recordings, or record of what you tap.
  • No name, email, account or other personal identifiers are attached.
  • Sentry processes reports on our behalf and does not use them for anything else.

DueHawk contains no advertising and no usage analytics. We don't track how you use the app.

DueHawk may show a small card recommending another NaRiSystems app. It is a plain link to Google Play; nothing about you is sent when it's shown or tapped.

🧒 Children's Privacy

DueHawk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact narisystems@gmail.com and we will delete it.

⚖️ Your Rights & Choices

  • Delete everything on your device: uninstall DueHawk, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → DueHawk.
  • Delete your backups: see the steps in §4.
  • Stop backups: turn off Google Drive backup in DueHawk's Settings.
  • Crash reports: these contain no personal data, so there is nothing to identify you by. If you have a question about them, email us.

Because NaRiSystems holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side. Your data is under your control on your device and in your Google account.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If DueHawk starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change ships. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
